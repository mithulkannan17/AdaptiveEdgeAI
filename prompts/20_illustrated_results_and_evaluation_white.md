# Poster Graphic Prompt 20: Comprehensive Results & Model Evaluation Dashboard (Academic Standard with Data Visuals)

## Overview
This visual represents the **Results & Evaluation** section of an academic engineering poster. It combines clean scientific charts, an illustrated Confusion Matrix heatmap, and key benchmark metrics on a **pure clean solid white background (#FFFFFF)**, structured to look like a published IEEE/Nature research results board (not a commercial ad).

---

## 🎨 Option 1: Landscape Format (`--ar 16:9` — Horizontal Results Board)

```text
An academic scientific results and performance evaluation board for an Edge AI forest monitoring platform, isolated on a pure clean solid white background (#FFFFFF).

The visual is structured into a clean top-to-bottom layout of rounded white modular cards with subtle drop shadows and crisp colored borders:

1. TOP SECTION [HERO KPI PERFORMANCE SCORECARD]:
- A horizontal strip of four clean metric pill badges with bold typography:
  [Accuracy: 97.2%] (Overall multi-class threat accuracy) |
  [Edge Latency: 42ms] (On-device neural inference) |
  [False-Alarm Rejection: 99.1%] (CADIE weather noise suppression) |
  [Energy Autonomy: 24/7] (Solar MPPT continuous harvesting)

2. BOTTOM SECTION [THREE HIGH-IMPACT SCIENTIFIC EVALUATION CARDS]:
- Card 1 (Left) [CONFUSION MATRIX HEATMAP & ACOUSTIC CLASSIFICATION]:
  - A clean 6x6 Confusion Matrix grid showing acoustic classification accuracy across key forest threats: Chainsaw (97%), Gunshot (98%), Fire Crackle (95%), Human Voice/Footsteps (94%), Wildlife (96%), Ambient (98%).
  - Clean teal-to-navy diagonal gradient representing high accuracy, with near-zero off-diagonal false positives, accompanied by a miniature audio mel-spectrogram visual snippet above the matrix.

- Card 2 (Center) [NOISE ROBUSTNESS & ENVIRONMENTAL ADAPTATION CURVE]:
  - A scientific dual-line graph: "Model Accuracy vs Environmental Noise (SNR dB)".
  - Compares: A solid emerald green curve labeled "AuraForest (CADIE Adaptive)" maintaining >95% accuracy even in heavy tropical rainstorms (0–10 dB SNR), versus a dropping red dashed line labeled "Fixed Baseline Model" falling below 60%.
  - Crisp, legible axes with labeled gridlines.

- Card 3 (Right) [LATENCY & TIME EFFICIENCY COMPARISON]:
  - A clean comparative bar chart comparing processing speed:
    • A small, bright cyan bar labeled "AuraForest On-Device Edge AI: 42 ms (Zero Cloud Bandwidth)"
    • A tall red bar labeled "Cloud Satellite Systems: 4,500 ms (High Latency & Cellular Reliance)"
  - Side callout badge: "107x Faster Response Time // Instant Threat Interception".

STYLE: Nature / IEEE journal experimental results layout, clean vector charts, razor-sharp typography, pure solid white background, vibrant teal, emerald, and coral accents, zero commercial advertising styling, publication standard, 8K resolution --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.0
```
