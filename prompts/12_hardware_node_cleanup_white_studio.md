# Poster Graphic Prompt 12: Hardware Node Prototype Polish & White Studio Cleanup

## Overview
This prompt is specially designed to transform physical prototype photos (which may have rough DIY cutouts, uneven hole drilling, visible seams, or inconsistent plastic colors) into **sleek, factory-manufactured industrial product photographs** isolated on a **pure solid white background (#FFFFFF)**.

---

## 🛠️ How to Use With Your Own Prototype Photos (Image-to-Image / Midjourney)

If you have actual photos of your 4 prototype nodes and want AI to **keep your exact shape while cleaning up the holes and giving it a white background**:

### In Midjourney:
1. Upload your photo to Discord (press `+` and upload).
2. Right-click / tap the uploaded photo and click **"Copy Link"**.
3. Type `/imagine` and paste your photo URL first, followed by this prompt:
   ```text
   [PASTE_YOUR_PHOTO_URL_HERE] professional industrial design product cleanup, perfect smooth CNC cutouts, seamless matte olive drab and graphite finish, clean hydrophobic acoustic mesh, isolated on pure solid white background (#FFFFFF), studio soft drop shadow, commercial hardware photography, 8k --iw 1.8 --v 6.0
   ```
   *(Note: `--iw 1.8` ensures Midjourney stays extremely loyal to your actual prototype's physical shape while fixing the colors, holes, and background).*

### In Leonardo AI / DALL-E / Photoshop Generative Fill:
- Select **Image Guidance / Image-to-Image**, set strength to `0.35` (preserves your shape while re-texturing the surfaces and cutouts), and use Prompt 2 above.
