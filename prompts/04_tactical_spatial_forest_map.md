# Poster Graphic Prompt 04: Spatial Tactical Forest Node Deployment Map

## Overview
This visual illustrates the spatial deployment geography of **AuraForest Sentinel** across a real protected reserve sector. It explicitly highlights:
- **Sentinel Tree Nodes** (Cyan/Emerald luminous pulsing dots across the canopy mesh)
- **Forest Ranger Units** (Electric Blue & Gold tactical shield dots on patrol routes)
- **Forest Access Roads & Trails** (Grey gravel logging roads and paved patrol highway)
- **Public & Village Settlement Zone** (Warm Amber/Orange clustered perimeter dots at the forest boundary)
- **Active Incident Hotzone** (Crimson radar beacon indicating acoustic threat origin)

4. ACTIVE THREAT EPICENTER (CRIMSON RED STROBE): Deep in Sector 7, a vivid crimson radar pulse wave radiating outward from a dense grove, labeled "Chainsaw Incident Pinpointed".

STYLE: High-end architectural visualization, soft ambient studio drop-shadow underneath the terrain block, pure white studio background, crisp technical callouts, razor-sharp 8k resolution, tilt-shift lens, National Geographic scientific standard --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.0
