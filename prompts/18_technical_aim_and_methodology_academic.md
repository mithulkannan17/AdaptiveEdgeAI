# Poster Graphic Prompt 18: Rigorous Academic Aim & Methodology Diagrams (No Ad/Marketing Look)

## Overview
These prompts generate **formal, rigorous academic research diagrams** for the **Aim & Objectives** and **Methodology** sections of an engineering project poster or paper. They use clean 2D vector wireframe boxes, standardized research notation, and pure white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`) with zero commercial advertising or glossy marketing effects.

---

## 🎨 PROMPT 1: Academic Research Aim & Objectives Diagram (`--ar 16:9` or `--ar 3:4`)

```text
A formal academic research objectives diagram for an engineering capstone project called "AuraForest Sentinel", isolated on a pure clean solid white background (#FFFFFF).

The diagram presents a clean, structured 2D architectural objective matrix (IEEE conference research poster standard), organized into three flat rectangular wireframe boxes with muted colored borders and legible technical typography:

1. PRIMARY RESEARCH AIM [Top Header Banner - Deep Slate Gray]:
   - "CENTRAL AIM: Autonomous On-Device Edge AI Platform for Real-Time Wilderness Acoustic Defense & Poaching Prevention in Zero-Connectivity Forest Ecosystems"

2. THREE CORE RESEARCH OBJECTIVES [Three Clean Columns with Thin Muted Outlines]:
   - Column 1 [Objective 1 - Forest Green Border]:
     "O1: ZERO-CLOUD PERCEPTION & INFERENCE"
     • Continuous 24/7 on-device acoustic surveillance using low-power dual-core microcontrollers
     • Sub-50ms inference latency running fully offline without satellite or cellular network dependency
     • Ultra-low power consumption with solar MPPT micro-energy harvesting

   - Column 2 [Objective 2 - Technical Cyan Border]:
     "O2: MULTIMODAL CONTEXT & FALSE-ALARM REJECTION"
     • Real-time fusion of 16kHz I2S digital audio with analog gas and seismic sensor telemetry
     • Adaptive dynamic thresholding (CADIE) to suppress rain, wind, and storm acoustic false positives
     • Open-set novelty detection to isolate unknown acoustic anomalies

   - Column 3 [Objective 3 - Crimson Red Border]:
     "O3: TACTICAL FIELD ACTION & TRIAGE"
     • Automated multi-stakeholder incident routing based on threat severity scores
     • Millisecond dispatch coordinates to Chief Ranger Command HQ and Field Patrol GPS units
     • Early civilian hazard warning for border communities (wildfire smoke & wildlife buffer breach)

STYLE: Authentic academic paper figure, minimalist flat 2D vector boxes with very subtle light pastel fills, crisp high-contrast black typography, clean lines, pure solid white background, zero 3D glossy plastic, zero advertising aesthetics, IEEE publication standard, 8K resolution --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.0
```

## 🎨 PROMPT 2: Rigorous Scientific Methodology Pipeline Flowchart (`--ar 16:9` or `--ar 3:4`)

```text
A formal scientific methodology workflow flowchart for an Edge AI IoT engineering research project, isolated on a pure clean solid white background (#FFFFFF).

A structured, horizontal 4-phase sequential engineering methodology pipeline connected by clean orthogonal black vector flow arrows (systems engineering standard):

PHASE 1: MULTIMODAL DATA ACQUISITION & SIGNAL CONDITIONING [Box 1 - Soft Mint Fill]:
- Technical processes listed inside box:
  • Raw 16 kHz I2S digital acoustic stream acquisition via INMP441 MEMS microphone
  • Atmospheric sampling: MQ-2 smoke and MQ-135 VOC sensor ADC quantization
  • Time-frequency conversion: 64-band Mel-Filterbank Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT)
  • Environmental baseline calibration: Ambient wind, temperature, and humidity tracking

PHASE 2: ON-DEVICE NEURAL CLASSIFICATION & QUANTIZATION [Box 2 - Soft Cyan Fill]:
- Technical processes listed inside box:
  • Quantized INT8 deep acoustic neural network feature extraction
  • Multi-class threat probability vector calculation (chainsaw, gunshot, wildfire, human activity)
  • Open-Set Unknown Discovery: Dirichlet process clustering for novel sound classification
  • Memory constraint optimization: Compressed to <4MB footprint for microcontrollers

PHASE 3: CADIE DYNAMIC CONTEXTUAL VERIFICATION [Box 3 - Soft Blue Fill]:
- Technical processes listed inside box:
  • Real-time Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) estimation in dynamic weather
  • Dynamic classification threshold shifting (noise-compensating sensitivity adaptation)
  • Multimodal cross-sensor validation: Correlating sound energy with smoke/gas risk indices
  • False-alarm elimination rate: >99% verified suppression under tropical storm conditions

PHASE 4: TACTICAL EVALUATION & TRI-TIER FIELD DISPATCH [Box 4 - Soft Coral Fill]:
- Technical processes listed inside box:
  • Tactical decision scoring & risk level calculation (Nominal / Elevated / Critical)
  • Priority dispatch routing via local LoRa mesh & REST API telemetry
  • Synchronized multi-stakeholder delivery: Chief Ranger GIS, Field Ranger GPS, Public Warning
  • End-to-end incident turnaround latency: <1.8 seconds from acoustic onset to alert

STYLE: IEEE / Springer scientific journal methodology flowchart, flat 2D rectangular process blocks, crisp standardized black text, clean directional arrows, pure solid white background, no 3D elements, no glossy ads, publication standard, 8K resolution --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.0
```
