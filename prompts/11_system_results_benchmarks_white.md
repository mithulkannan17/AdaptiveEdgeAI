# Poster Graphic Prompt 11: System Results & Performance Benchmarks

## Overview
This visual represents the **System Results & Experimental Evaluation** section of the project poster. It showcases empirical benchmarks proving the real-world performance of AuraForest Sentinel:
1. **Hero KPI Scorecard**: Overall Accuracy (97.2%), Edge Latency (<42ms), False Alarm Suppression (99.1%), and Power Longevity (24/7 Solar Autonomy).
2. **Graph 1 (Noise Robustness Curve)**: CADIE Dynamic Adaptation vs. Fixed Baseline under escalating tropical rain/wind background noise.
3. **Graph 2 (Cloud vs. Edge Latency Bar Chart)**: Traditional Cloud Systems (4500ms+ latency, dependent on cellular network) vs. AuraForest Edge AI (42ms on-device execution).
4. **Graph 3 (Multimodal Threat Response Timeline)**: Detection-to-Dispatch timeline proving <1.8s incident response.
5. **Background**: Pure solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with modular rounded container cards and clean vector graphs.
